# 论文成果

## MSA

1. [**HAlign 3**](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msac166): fast multiple alignment of ultra-large numbers of similar DNA/RNA sequences. **Molecular Biology and Evolution**. 2022 ([soft](https://github.com/malabz/HAlign-3))

2. [**HAlign 4**](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/40/12/btae718/7912339): a new strategy for rapidly aligning millions of sequences. **Bioinformatics**. 2024 ([soft](https://github.com/malabz/HAlign-4))

数据集推荐:适合处理序列数量极多、长度中等、整体相似度较高的数据集。

3. [**FMAlign2**](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae014): a novel fast multiple nucleotide sequence alignment method for ultralong datasets. **Bioinformatics**. 2024 ([soft](https://github.com/malabz/FMAlign2))

数据集推荐:适合序列数量少，但单条序列长度很长，且整体相似度较高的数据集。

4. [**minipoa**](https://genome.cshlp.org/content/early/2026/07/23/gr282046126): Fast and memory efficient partial order alignment with minipoa. **Genome Research**. 2026 ([soft](https://github.com/NCl3-lhd/minipoa))

数据集推荐:适合序列数量中等，序列长度长，且整体相似度在中等以上的数据集。
